1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & SYSTEM MISSION
================================================================================
AURA-TM (Autonomous Urban Routing & Adaptive Traffic Management) is a deterministic,
predictive mobility decision-support engine deployed within a Smart City Integrated
Command and Control Centre (ICCC). 

Modern urban expressways suffer from acute vulnerability to compounding disruptions:
heterogeneous traffic fleet mixes, monsoon rainfall, localized waterlogging, and
extreme peak commute surges. AURA-TM replaces ad-hoc human guesswork with an
auditable, mathematically grounded forecasting engine.

Core Directives:
1. Multi-Variable Telemetry Ingestion: Continuously ingests 15-minute vehicle fleet
   counts, weather sensor data, road surface reports, and historical baselines.
2. Deterministic Demand Forecasting: Forecasts hourly traffic demand across rolling
   horizons (T0, T+15m, T+30m, and T+60m) using calibrated temporal multipliers.
3. Capacity Degradation Modeling: Dynamically throttles road capacity based on
   micro-climatic precipitation and surface waterlogging telemetry.
4. Statistical Anomaly Surveillance: Evaluates demand variance against historical
   time-matched distributions using the Congestion Deviation Index (CDI).
5. Safety-Gated Advisory Issuance: Operates strictly in ADVISORY_ONLY mode by
   default, withholding physical traffic signal commands unless validated controller
   safety timing constraints and authorization credentials are provided.

3. COMPLETE GLOSSARY OF TERMS & ABBREVIATIONS

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 CORE SYSTEM & OPERATIONAL ACRONYMS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* ICCC (Integrated Command and Control Centre):
  The centralized nerve center of a modern smart city that integrates emergency
  services, traffic mobility, municipal utilities, disaster management, and public
  safety data into a single operational interface.

* AURA-TM (Autonomous Urban Routing & Adaptive Traffic Management):
  The decision-support and predictive engine that models traffic physics, detects
  anomalies, and recommends traffic management plans.

* ATCS (Adaptive Traffic Control System):
  Computerized signal control software (e.g., SCATS, SCOOT, Sydney Coordinated
  Adaptive Traffic System) that dynamically optimizes signal green split times,
  offsets, and cycle lengths based on real-time detector inputs.

* VMS (Variable Message Sign / Changeable Message Sign):
  High-intensity LED matrix electronic message gantries suspended above highways
  used to broadcast regulatory speeds, route divergence instructions, and hazard
  warnings to approaching motorists.

* CCTV (Closed-Circuit Television):
  High-definition, pan-tilt-zoom (PTZ) optical cameras mounted along the corridor
  providing video verification of queues, accidents, and waterlogging.

* ANPR (Automatic Number Plate Recognition):
  Optical cameras coupled with AI character recognition that identify vehicle
  registration plates and classify vehicle types at highway speeds.

* DRY_RUN / ADVISORY_ONLY:
  A protected operational state where the algorithm executes all calculations and
  generates advisories, but is mathematically barred from sending physical signal
  override commands to field intersection controllers.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2 TELEMETRY & HETEROGENEOUS FLEET TERMINOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Fleet Mix (Heterogeneous Traffic):
  Traffic conditions typical of Indian and Asian cities where roads are shared by
  wildly diverse vehicle types possessing radically different acceleration, braking,
  dimensions, and maneuverability characteristics.

* PCU (Passenger Car Unit / Passenger Car Equivalent - PCE):
  A universal traffic engineering conversion metric that translates heterogeneous
  vehicles into equivalent standard passenger cars. One passenger car equals 1.0 PCU.

* 2W (Two-Wheelers - Motorcycles, Scooters, Mopeds):
  Equivalent factor: 0.50 PCU/vehicle.
  Highly agile, occupies minimal road surface, can filter between lanes, but easily
  destabilized by wet asphalt and standing water.

* 3W (Three-Wheelers - Auto-rickshaws, Tempo-rickshaws):
  Equivalent factor: 0.80 PCU/vehicle.
  Intermediate vehicle with low acceleration and top speed; acts as a localized
  bottleneck during merge maneuvers.

* 4W (Four-Wheelers - Passenger Cars, Cabs, Sedans, Hatchbacks, SUVs):
  Equivalent factor: 1.00 PCU/vehicle.
  The baseline reference passenger vehicle.

* LCV (Light Commercial Vehicles - Delivery Vans, Mini-Trucks):
  Equivalent factor: 1.50 PCU/vehicle.
  Commercial freight vehicles with slower acceleration and higher road footprint.

* HCV (Heavy Commercial Vehicles - Multi-Axle Freight Trucks, Transit Buses):
  Equivalent factor: 3.00 PCU/vehicle.
  Large commercial vehicles requiring extensive stopping distance, wide turning
  sweeps, and substantial gap acceptance during merges.

* 15-Minute Observation Interval:
  The standard sampling time window (e.g., 18:00 to 18:15) used in advanced traffic
  telemetry to balance responsiveness against high-frequency statistical noise.

* Normalized Hourly Flow Rate (PCU/hr):
  The flow rate computed by multiplying the 15-minute PCU total by four (4). This
  standardizes demand onto an hourly scale for capacity comparison without falsely
  claiming an entire hour of physical observation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3 ROAD GEOMETRY & INFRASTRUCTURE TERMINOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Corridor-04-IT-Expressway-Merge:
  The specific urban highway link monitored by this dashboard, consisting of
  three (3) continuous mainline expressway lanes and one (1) left-hand merging
  slip lane connecting an IT business park.

* Mainline Carriageway:
  The primary through-lanes of the expressway carrying high-speed through traffic.

* Slip Lane / Merging Slip Road:
  An auxiliary deceleration/acceleration ramp allowing traffic from secondary
  arterials or business parks to join the mainline without a stop-controlled junction.

* Merge Gore / Chevron Marking:
  The triangular painted neutral zone bordered by white pavement markings located
  at the convergence of the slip lane and the mainline. Vehicles are legally
  prohibited from driving over gore markings.

* Upstream Roundabout:
  A major circular intersection situated 1.2 kilometers upstream from the merge
  point. When downstream expressway merges become congested, queues back up into
  the roundabout, locking up perpendicular city arterials.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4 ENVIRONMENTAL & WEATHER PARAMETERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Precipitation Rate (mm/hr):
  The volume of rain falling per unit time, measured by optical or acoustic rain
  sensors.
  - Clear / Dry : 0.0 mm/hr
  - Light Rain  : 0.1 to 15.0 mm/hr
  - Heavy Rain  : > 15.0 mm/hr (Monsoon downpour)

* Horizontal Visibility (m):
  The maximum distance (in meters) at which a prominent black object can be seen
  and identified against the horizon. Heavy rain and fog reduce visibility,
  compelling drivers to increase following headway.

* Standing Water / Waterlogging:
  Water accumulation on the road surface exceeding 15 mm in depth. Causes tire
  hydroplaning, vehicle avoidance swerving, and lane abandonment.

* WCM (Weather Capacity Multiplier):
  A dimensionless reduction factor (0.55 to 1.00) applied to design capacity:
  - 1.00 : Clear / Dry (Precipitation = 0 mm/hr, Visibility >= 1000 m)
  - 0.88 : Light Rain / Mist (Precipitation 0.1-15.0 mm/hr, Visibility >= 500 m)
  - 0.65 : Heavy Rain / Waterlogging (Precipitation > 15.0 mm/hr OR Standing Water)
  - 0.55 : Dense Fog / Severe Storm (Visibility < 150 m)

* Effective Capacity (PCU/hr):
  The maximum hourly rate at which vehicles can traverse the roadway under prevailing
  weather conditions. Calculated as: Base Design Capacity * WCM.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.5 TEMPORAL CONTEXT & CALENDAR MODIFIERS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Active Temporal Window:
  Standardized time bands reflecting city commute rhythms:
  1. Morning Peak Build-Up    (08:00 - 09:29) : Inbound commuter surge
  2. Morning Peak Plateau     (09:30 - 10:59) : High sustained commute flow
  3. Mid-Day Off-Peak         (11:00 - 16:59) : Normal steady commercial demand
  4. Evening Peak Build-Up    (17:00 - 18:59) : Massive outbound employee departure
  5. Evening Peak Dissipation (19:00 - 21:29) : Tapering return trips
  6. Night Freight Window     (21:30 - 07:59) : Commercial heavy freight access

* TPM (Temporal Progression Multiplier):
  Dimensionless progression multipliers calibrated for horizons T+15m, T+30m, and
  T+60m relative to base demand T0.

* Rolling Horizons:
  - T0    : Current time interval (e.g., 18:15:00)
  - T+15m : Short-term outlook (18:30:00) - immediate tactical intervention horizon
  - T+30m : Medium-term outlook (18:45:00) - peak queue buildup horizon
  - T+60m : Extended outlook (19:15:00) - peak plateau / dissipation transition

* Calendar / Surge Adjustment (+0.08):
  An empirical uplift factor added to baseline TPMs when traffic is compounded by
  weekend departure behavior or civic events.

* Anti-Double Counting Rule:
  Per Protocol B.3, if an input contains multiple overlapping qualifiers (such as
  "Friday" AND "Pre-Weekend Outbound Surge"), the +0.08 adjustment is applied
  strictly ONCE per horizon.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.6 TRAFFIC FLOW DYNAMICS & CAPACITY METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Base V/C Ratio (Volume-to-Capacity):
  Normalized demand divided by dry Base Design Capacity.

* Effective V/C Ratio:
  Normalized demand divided by Weather-Adjusted Effective Capacity. Indicates
  true operational congestion pressure.

* [OVERSATURATED_REGIME] (V/C > 1.00):
  The operating condition where vehicle arrival rate exceeds departure service
  rate. Vehicles accumulate in physical queues, travel speeds collapse, and flow
  transitions to stop-and-go shockwaves.
  IMPORTANT NOTE: V/C is a flow ratio, NOT physical road surface occupancy.

* Upstream Roundabout Spillback Threshold (V/C > 1.15):
  An empirical threshold for Corridor-04. When Effective V/C exceeds 1.15 under
  heavy rain, queue propagation speed exceeds the clearance capacity of the
  upstream roundabout, resulting in complete gridlock of the feeding road network.

* LoS (Level of Service):
  A standardized qualitative measure describing operational conditions within a
  traffic stream, graded from LoS A (free flow) to LoS F (breakdown/oversaturation).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.7 STATISTICAL ANOMALY DETECTION METRICS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* CDI (Congestion Deviation Index):
  The statistical standard score (z-score) representing how many standard
  deviations current demand deviates from time-matched historical expectations.

* Historical Baseline Mean:
  The average demand observed across identical days of the week and identical time
  intervals over the preceding 6 to 12 months (e.g., Fridays 18:00 - 19:00).

* Historical Standard Deviation (sigma):
  The statistical dispersion of historical demand during the reference window.

* Statistical Interpretation Thresholds:
  - |CDI| < 1.00       : NORMAL_VARIATION
  - 1.00 <= CDI <= 1.80: ELEVATED_DEMAND_ANOMALY
  - CDI > 1.80         : SEVERE_POSITIVE_ANOMALY (Urgent field verification required)
  - CDI < -1.00        : LOW_DEMAND_ANOMALY (Sensor dropout or upstream total blockage)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.8 SAFETY-GATE & SIGNAL ACTUATION CONSTRAINTS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* Safety Gate:
  An algorithmic interlock designed to prevent automated systems from actuating
  physical signal hardware without validated safety constraints.

* Authorized Commands Array (`"authorized_commands": []`):
  The machine-to-machine payload field containing executable controller instructions.
  In AURA-TM, this array is strictly empty whenever safety gates are armed.

* Phase Split Timing Table:
  The programmed distribution of green time percentages allocated to competing
  movements within a traffic signal cycle.

* Phase Conflict Matrix:
  A safety-critical logic matrix that physically prevents conflicting vehicle
  movements from receiving simultaneous green indications.

* Minimum / Maximum Green Times:
  Absolute hard bounds on green signal duration ensuring minimum pedestrian
  clearance and preventing indefinite cycle hold.

* Yellow Change & All-Red Clearance Intervals:
  Mandatory safety intervals that clear the intersection conflict zone before
  cross-street movements are permitted to proceed.

* Downstream Storage Telemetry:
  Real-time occupancy measurements of the exit link. If downstream space is full,
  injecting additional green time causes "spillback gridlock".

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.9 DATA PROVENANCE CLASSIFICATION TAGS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every piece of data in AURA-TM is tagged to prevent synthetic data pollution:
* [MEASURED]                  : Directly obtained from physical sensors (e.g., ANPR, loops).
* [CALCULATED]                : Deterministically computed using declared formulas.
* [FORECAST]                  : Projected forward in time using temporal multipliers.
* [IMPUTED]                   : Synthesized to replace missing/failed sensor inputs.
* [DECLARED_STATIC_PARAMETER] : Fixed engineering reference value (e.g., base capacity).
* [HISTORICAL_BASELINE]       : Derived from long-term historical log distributions.
* [FIELD_REPORT]              : Submitted by on-ground patrols or emergency personnel.
* [UNAVAILABLE]               : Missing, unmodeled, or withheld per safety protocol.

4.1 MODULE 1: FLEET PCU CONVERSION & DEMAND NORMALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Input: 15-minute raw vehicle counts by class:
  Count_2W  = 520 vehicles / 15 min [MEASURED]
  Count_3W  = 140 vehicles / 15 min [MEASURED]
  Count_4W  = 580 vehicles / 15 min [MEASURED]
  Count_LCV = 44 vehicles / 15 min  [MEASURED]
  Count_HCV = 38 vehicles / 15 min  [MEASURED]

Formula 1: Current 15-Minute PCU Load
  Current_15m_Load = (Count_2W * 0.50) + (Count_3W * 0.80) + (Count_4W * 1.00)
                     + (Count_LCV * 1.50) + (Count_HCV * 3.00)
  Substitution     = (520 * 0.50) + (140 * 0.80) + (580 * 1.00)
                     + (44 * 1.50) + (38 * 3.00)
                   = 260.0 + 112.0 + 580.0 + 66.0 + 114.0
  Result           = 1132.0 PCU / 15 min [CALCULATED]

Formula 2: Normalized Hourly Flow Rate (T0)
  Normalized_Demand_T0 = Current_15m_Load * 4 (intervals / hr)
                       = 1132.0 * 4
  Result               = 4528.0 PCU/hr [CALCULATED]

Formula 3: Unadjusted Base V/C Ratio (T0)
  Unadjusted_Base_V_C = Normalized_Demand_T0 / Base_Design_Capacity
                      = 4528.0 / 4200.0
  Result              = 1.078095... -> 1.08 [CALCULATED]

5. TRAFFIC-CONTROL SAFETY GATE & COMMAND WITHHOLDING PROTOCOL
================================================================================
Why Automated Signal Actuation Is Blocked:
In an urban corridor, adjusting signal timing solely from corridor-level V/C
is extremely hazardous. Without knowing lane-by-lane storage occupancy, extending
mainline green times can pump vehicles directly into an already blocked bottleneck,
causing intersection gridlock and blocking emergency vehicles.

AURA-TM enforces strict command withholding:
1. "authorized_commands" MUST remain an empty array: []
2. Operating mode is permanently locked to "ADVISORY_ONLY / DRY_RUN"
3. Executable signals can ONLY be unlocked when the input provides:
   - Approved Signal Timing Sheet (Cycle length, phase splits)
   - Verified Phase Conflict Matrix
   - Minimum and Maximum Green bounds (preventing starvation)
   - Yellow and All-Red clearance times
   - Downstream queue storage telemetry
   - Cryptographic Operator Authorization Token

Instead of automated signal overrides, AURA-TM outputs prioritized human advisories:
- Urgent Drainage Pump Team dispatch to clear standing water on the slip lane.
- PTZ CCTV surveillance focused on upstream roundabout approaches to monitor spillback.
- Dynamic VMS broadcast recommendations advising drivers to divert via Outer Ring Road.

8. MACHINE-TO-MACHINE (M2M) JSON INTERFACE SPECIFICATION
================================================================================
The dashboard generates a strictly valid JSON payload for ingestion by smart city
ICCC message brokers (e.g., Apache Kafka, MQTT, REST webhooks).

Key Payload Schema Properties:
- `system_id`           : System identifier string ("AURA-TM-DETERMINISTIC-ENGINE")
- `version`             : Engine build version ("2.4.0")
- `timestamp_iso8601`   : Observation timestamp with explicit timezone offset
- `corridor_id`         : Standardized corridor ID ("CORRIDOR-04-IT-EXPRESSWAY-MERGE")
- `safety_mode`         : Operating safety mode ("ADVISORY_ONLY / DRY_RUN")
- `data_quality`        : Sensor health, validation checks, unmodeled attribute list
- `forecast_assumptions`: Calibration status and justification for temporal adjustments
- `model_limitations`   : Clear declaration of unmodeled metrics (speed, queue meters)
- `telemetry_summary`   : Raw vehicle counts, environmental readings, historical baselines
- `calculated_metrics_t0`: 15m load, normalized demand, capacities, V/C ratios, and LoS
- `statistical_anomaly_evaluation`: CDI z-score, anomaly class, and interpretation
- `forecast_horizons`   : Array of horizon projections (T+15m, T+30m, T+60m)
- `recommended_advisories`: Prioritized operational guidance for field teams
- `authorized_commands` : Mandatory empty array (`[]`) per safety gate protocol
